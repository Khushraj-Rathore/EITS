Do you offer a free IT Health Check? ⌄
Yes — we offer a free, no-obligation IT Health Check for any Fiji business. Our team reviews your current technology environment and provides a plain-English report identifying risks, inefficiencies, and recommendations. Zero cost, zero commitment.
What areas of Fiji do you service? ⌄
Our head office is in Suva and we provide on-site support across Suva, Nadi, Lautoka, and nationwide. For managed IT clients, we also provide 24/7 remote support from anywhere in Fiji — including outer islands via remote management tools.
How fast do you respond to IT issues? ⌄
For managed IT clients, we guarantee a 15-minute response to critical incidents, 24/7. For general enquiries submitted via this form, you'll hear from us within 1 business hour during office hours (Mon–Fri 8am–5pm).

Yes. EITS Fiji is one of Fiji's select Xero Adviser Certified partners. We handle everything from initial Xero setup and data migration to FRCS payroll compliance, staff training, and ongoing Xero advisory.
Can I request a free product demo? ⌄
Absolutely. We offer free, live demos for all our software products — Retail POS, Auto Parts POS, Inventory Management, Resort Management, Payroll, and Xero Accounting. Simply select the product you're interested in from the enquiry form above and we'll schedule a demo at your convenience.
Do you work with small businesses or only large enterprises? ⌄
We work with businesses of all sizes — from single-person startups to multi-location enterprises. Our solutions are scalable and priced to suit your business stage. Whether you're a small retail shop or a large hospitality group, we have the right solution for you.
